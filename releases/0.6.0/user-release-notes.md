# Schmegettes 0.6.0

## Hosting
- **Scan to join.** The lobby shows a QR code above the room code. Point a phone's camera at the TV and it opens the join screen for that room: no code to type.
- **The join address is up top.** "Join at schmegettes.app/join" sits at the top of the lobby, for anyone whose camera won't scan.
- **Fix anyone's name.** Open the player list and tap ✎ next to any name to change it before you start. The host's own name is at the bottom of the list.
- **A cleaner lobby.** The game options no longer show under the code; they're in ⚙ only.
- **Simpler options.** Difficulty is just Any, Easy, Medium or Hard. "Quick picks" is now **Default spoken clue value**: **Ask** (the default) or **Lowest left**, for when a player says just a category.

## Getting around
- **Home from anywhere.** Every screen has a ⌂ button. During a game it asks first, and you keep your seat: **Rejoin** on Home puts you back in. It waits while you're answering.
- **My games is one list.** The All / Played / Watched filter is gone; games you watched are still in the list, marked Watched.

## Your data
- **Guest data expires.** If you play without an account and don't come back for 90 days, that guest and its games are deleted. Signed-in accounts are kept.

## Looks
- **A new icon** for the browser tab and the Home Screen.
