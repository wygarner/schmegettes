# Schmegettes 0.8.0

## Answering
- **More time to start.** After you win the buzz you have 6 seconds to start your answer, counted from when your phone is actually listening, then 6 more to finish. "Um…" doesn't count as starting.
- **Pausing is fine.** A pause mid-answer no longer cuts you off.
- **Fairer judging.** The same words in a different order or form count: "cave exploration" for *Exploring Caves*. Silent letters don't trip you up: "nee" for *Knee*.

## Hosting
- **Overrides end the clue.** If the host marks an earlier answer right, later answers on that clue don't count, win or lose. They show **DOESN'T COUNT**.
- **The TV keeps its sound.** If the host's device loses sound (switching apps, a call), it comes back by itself, or the game pauses until the host taps **Tap to turn on sound**.

## Fixes
- Scores reset to $0 as soon as a rematch starts.
- On Android phones, the keyboard no longer covers the box you're typing in.
- The wager pad won't let you type more than you can bet.
