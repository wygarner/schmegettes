# Schmegettes 0.2.0

## Narration
- **The narrator is always a Schmegettes voice** (Kristin, Norman or John), on every host device, and it's heard even with the iPhone's silent switch on.
- **Start waits for the voice** to finish downloading the first time, and shows its progress.

## Playing
- **A snappier pick.** The chosen clue blinks on the board and grows into a card with its category and value, then the clue is read. No more "Here's … for …".
- **Less waiting after a clue.** The board comes back a second after the correct response is read.
- **Five categories per board**, so the board is easier to read on the TV.
- **Sound effects**: a chime when someone gets it, a time's-up tone when nobody does, a flourish for Daily Doubles, a fanfare for Double Schmegettes, and a thunk every time you press your buzzer.

## Lobby
- **Game options**: pick which seasons the clues come from, the pace (Relaxed, Standard or Fast), and whether to play Double Schmegettes.
- **Microphone first**: you allow the microphone before joining, so no permission pop-up interrupts a game.
- **Full screen on iPhone**: the host lobby shows how to add Schmegettes to your Home Screen, which hides Safari's toolbar.

## Getting back in
- **Rejoin**: closed the tab by accident? Home shows your game in progress. Tap Rejoin to get your seat and score back.
- **Resume**: a game that ended unfinished can be picked up again by its host within 30 days, with the same board and scores.
- Opening your game on another phone moves you there.
