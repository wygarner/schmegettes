# Schmegettes 0.1.0

The first release of Schmegettes, the quiz show for your living room.

## Play
- **Host on the TV, play on your phones.** The host device runs the board; everyone else joins with a five-letter room code. Up to 6 players, and the host can play too.
- **Buzz in and answer out loud.** A big red buzzer on every phone, 9 seconds to buzz and 9 to answer. Answers that sound right count.
- **A narrator reads the clues**, with three Schmegettes voices to choose from in the lobby, and says the correct response after each clue.
- **Daily Doubles and Final Schmegettes**, with wagers, plus Double Schmegettes in round two.
- **Fresh boards** from the real clue catalog, avoiding clues your group has already seen.

## After the game
- A final scoreboard, **Rematch**, and fixes for the host when a verdict was wrong.
- **My games** keeps every game you played or watched, with your games, wins and best score, and filters for played and watched games.

## Accounts
- Play as a guest, or create an account with email and password to keep your games and host access on any phone.

## Hosting
- Hosting is invite-only for now: ask for the current host code.
